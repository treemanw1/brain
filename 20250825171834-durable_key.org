#+title: Durable Key

Durable or durable supernatural keys are keys tagged to some entity that are
persistent and which do not change unlike their [[id:1f23a0f3-e867-4a77-91dd-419d66f034e9][natural key]] counterparts.

While multiple [[id:0e4f9317-012d-4f98-8e4b-94c411b9b77e][surrogate keys]] may be associated with an entity, e.g. an employee
over time, the durable key never changes.
