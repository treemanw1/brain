#+title: OLAP Cube

[[id:391f14b8-8da4-4d36-8171-f0aebd18a9b2][Materialized view]] that is a grid of precomputed aggregates grouped by different
dimensions.

e.g. Two-dimensional grid of products and dates. So every cell can store the sum
of an attribute, say ~net_price~, for that specific product and date.
