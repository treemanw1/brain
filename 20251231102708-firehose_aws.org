#+title: Firehose (AWS)

Managed service for delivering real-time streaming data to destinations e.g. S3,
Redshift, HTTP endpoints.

* Firehose vs MSK Sink Connect

- Firehose can ingest from a myriad of sources whereas MSK Sink Connect connects
purely to a MSK cluster.
