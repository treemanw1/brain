#+title: Star Schema

[[id:61fa7b12-a4cf-41f2-98f6-732410f362aa][Database schema]] where data is structured to be easily understood and queried
fast, usually for reporting/dashboard use cases.

Comprises of a central [[id:e9a7cb26-87d7-4b86-bb25-f7cdc4538df1][fact table]] containing measurable business data, e.g.
sales, clicks, revenue. Adajacent are [[id:5636a488-dd17-4a92-befd-d16eaa0a526d][denormalized]] [[id:5665111d-8d5c-4c3c-a696-0983ce2cbd95][dimension tables]] adding
context to each fact, e.g. customers, products.

* Pros

- Faster queries due to lesser joins required to retrieve data.

* Cons

- Increased storage due to data redundancy
- Maintenance challenges due to increased complexity (that increases with the
  complexity of data hierarchies) to update data
