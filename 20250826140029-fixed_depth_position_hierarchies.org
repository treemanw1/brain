#+title: Fixed Depth Positional Hierarchy

[[id:2146c531-4313-45bd-8850-038009be5744][Dimensional hierarchy]] where the number of levels is known and does not change,
and where each level has its own column in the [[id:5665111d-8d5c-4c3c-a696-0983ce2cbd95][dimension table]].

e.g. Day -> Month -> Quarter -> Year
