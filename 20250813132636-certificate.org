#+title: Certificate

Combination of Certificate data (public key, identity information, etc.) and the
digital signature.

* How is the digital signature generated?

Certificate data (public key, identity info, validity dates, extensions, etc.)
is hashed and then encrypted with the CA private key.

* How is the certificate verified?

The signature is verified by decrypting the signature with the CA's public key
and comparing that hash with the hashed certificate data in the certificate.
