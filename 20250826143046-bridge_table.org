#+title: Bridge Table

Table that tracks relationships between entities.

Example:

#+BEGIN_SRC text
    DimEmployee:
    | EmployeeKey | EmployeeName |
    |-------------+--------------|
    | 1           | Alice        |
    | 2           | Bob          |
    | 3           | Carol        |
    | 4           | Dave         |

    BridgeTable_EmployeeHierarchy:
    | AncestorKey | DescendantKey | Weight |
    |------------+---------------+--------|
    | 1          | 2             | 1      |
    | 1          | 3             | 2      |
    | 2          | 3             | 1      |
    | 1          | 4             | 1      |
    | 4          | 3             | 1      |
#+END_SRC
