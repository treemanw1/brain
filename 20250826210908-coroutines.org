#+title: Coroutines

Method of achieving [[id:7becff42-22af-4528-b940-5ebbedb25ab4][asynchrony]] through functions that can be paused and resumed
without hogging [[id:87685450-9446-49dd-aee0-0ae8928c59f2][threads]].

Requires some scheduler or [[id:221f21f0-a806-4840-8c91-dc193c9710b6][event loop]] to drive return of control and yielding to
and from coroutines.

* Examples

- Python's [[id:97014a42-7491-48cd-bcc8-92e04f5c552d][asyncio]]
