#+title: Consumer Acknowledgement and Publisher Confirms (RabbitMQ)

Mechanism for delivery and processing confirmation of messages as they are not
guaranteed to reach peers in a distributed system.

* Consumer Acknowledgement Modes

1. Automatic

Message is considered received right after it is sent.

2. Manual

Message is considered received after the node receives an acknowledgement from
the consumer.
