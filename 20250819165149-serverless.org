#+title: Serverless

[[id:627ee6ad-00e3-4b42-b4d2-2ecd5712d4c2][Cloud-computing]] execution model where the cloud provider manages infrastructure,
enabling developers to focus on writing code.

Often used for event-driven workloads and microservices.
