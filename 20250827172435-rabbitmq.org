#+title: RabbitMQ

[[id:d1dde541-656f-4ecf-9f86-df520e3e850d][Message broker]] written in [[id:64693ac5-642c-40da-8682-aed943fa15d4][Erlang]] that manages, routes, and delivers messages
between producers and consumers.

Can be used with a variety of protocols including [[id:deaa9650-b870-4367-89e7-964726401c3c][AMQP]], STOMP, MQTT, and others.

* Concepts

- [[id:03f9d519-2d05-4330-84ea-7fcbd3213329][Exchange]]

Streams: Persistent replicated data structure that stores messages in an
append-only log that can be repeatedly read until expiration, similar to Kafka.

- [[id:b527848e-d9ba-48d6-98f1-ad7373dac781][Queue]]
- [[id:a9e6b177-3aaa-44b8-a013-544404de8c46][Bindings]]
- [[id:81987ef0-b1fb-441c-9061-d435e22e24c6][Consumer Acknowledgement and Publisher Confirms]]

* Use cases

/Messaging/ - low-latency and flexible delivery patterns.

1. Long-running tasks to reliably run background jobs
2. Inter-microservice communication
