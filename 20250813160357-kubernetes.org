#+title: Kubernetes

Container orchestration platform.

* What is the use case for Kubernetes?

Kubernetes is great at handling the operational overhead introduced by
[[id:d245ddbe-8b95-4b30-aa1a-911677fdc231][Microservices]], specifically through:

- Service discovery
- Load balancing
- Rolling updates
- Self-healing
- Horizontal scaling
