#+title: Liquid Clustering

[[https://docs.databricks.com/aws/en/tables/clustering][Data layout optimization technique]] built on [[id:ed073e7e-3b4e-4385-ac27-6b1fab8d33fe][Delta Lake]] that replaces [[id:4b3221ca-f37c-4a92-9aa3-29116154c3d0][table
partitioning]] and [[id:5c91ca7b-efbf-4e28-9036-4a93646112f3][z-ordering]]. 

Simplifies table management and optimizes query performance by automatically
organizing data based on clustering keys.
