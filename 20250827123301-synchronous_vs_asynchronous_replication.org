#+title: Synchronous vs Asynchronous Replication

In [[id:40f1985e-7c82-402b-847b-38733fda2d82][leader-based replication]], asynchronous replication is preferred.

With synchronous replication, followes are guaranteed to have up-to-date data
consistent with the leader. However, if a single follower fails, no writes can
be made by the leader until the follower is available again. Not very practical.

In practice synchronous replication involves making /one/ follower synchronous,
so that there is an up-to-date copy on at least two nodes. Sometimes called
semi-synchronous.

Asynchronous replication has the benefit of processing writes even if followers
are not updated (i.e. without waiting for all followers to be updated), but if
the leader fails, writes not yet replicated are lost.
