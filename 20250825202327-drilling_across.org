#+title: Drilling Across

aka stitch, multipass query

Term used to refer to making separate queries against two or more [[id:e9a7cb26-87d7-4b86-bb25-f7cdc4538df1][fact tables]] by
performing a sort-merge operation on the common [[id:5665111d-8d5c-4c3c-a696-0983ce2cbd95][dimension]] row headers.
