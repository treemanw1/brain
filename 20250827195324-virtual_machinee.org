#+title: Virtual Machine (disambiguation)

Virtualization or emulation of a computer system. May refer to either:

1. [[id:81f046e6-fc7e-4d1b-b68c-acf13d92a265][System virtual machines]] a.k.a. full virtualization VMs that virtualize an
   entire machine including the OS.

2. [[id:9a650fe7-0963-41fa-914c-8498c677731e][Process virtual machines]] that are designed to execute programs in a
   platform-independent environment.
