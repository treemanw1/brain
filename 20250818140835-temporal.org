#+title: Temporal

[[id:0c61cbe1-1eee-4e91-bed4-9d56198cf15f][Workflow orchestration]] engine that ensures durable execution.

Developed at Uber to orchestrate long-running, stateful, failure-prone business
processes in a distributed environment.

* How is Temporal different from [[id:3147c4ea-02fb-4009-81b6-4898522a2f44][AWS Step Functions]]?

AWS Step Functions utilise a state machine persisted by AWS to checkpoint steps,
so retries are performed at a step level. The worker code lives outside of Step
Functions, usually Lambda or ECS tasks.

Temporal's workflow logic itself is durable and replayable, because every event
(function call, timer, signal) is recorded in a database, so retries can be
performed "in the middle of code" rather than just at a step level.
