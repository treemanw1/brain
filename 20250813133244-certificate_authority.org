#+title: Certificate Authority

Trusted third party that verifies the validity of public keys.

* Why are CAs needed?

Without CAs, malicious attackers are able to perform man-in-the-middle attacks
by intercepting communications and substituting content with their own key pair,
after which they are able to decrypt and alter messages.

CAs prevent this by performing an additional check on public keys via their
trusted public key which is usually pre-installed in the OS or browser.
