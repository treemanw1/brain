#+title: Persist (Spark)

Mechanism for optimizing performance by caching RDDs/DataFrames in memory or on
disk to avoid reomputation for subsequent actions.

~persist~: Enables developer to specify ~StorageLevel~, e.g. ~MEMORY_ONLY~,
~DISK_ONLY~, ~MEMORY_AND_DISK_SER~

~cache~: Shorthand for ~persist~ with ~MEMORY_AND_DISK_SER~
