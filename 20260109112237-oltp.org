#+title: OLTP

Online Transactional Processing refers to systems specifically designed for
large volumes of short, fast, real-time transactions.

Prioritizes speed, concurrency, and reliability.

Applications typically interact with OTLP systems by requesting retrieval of
records using a key, which the storage engine searches for with an index.
Typically disk seek time is the bottleneck here.
