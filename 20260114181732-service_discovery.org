#+title: Service Discovery  

The problem where a client has to determine which node to connect to when
interacting with a highly available service with multiple redundant nodes.

* Approaches

1. Allow clients to connect to any node.
2. Send all requests to routing tier first, forwards to the relevant node.
3. Require clients to be aware of partitioning and assignment of partitions to
   nodes.
