#+title: Message Broker

Service that manages the routing, delivery, and persistence of messages between
producers and consumers.

Supports multiple queues, [[id:110b962b-36fc-484e-aafe-7367e21cd7af][topics]], streams and is capable of fan-out. Handles
persistence, retries, ordering, and sometimes filtering.
