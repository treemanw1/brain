#+title: One Big Table (OBT)

Also known as wide denormalized tables.

Data modeling technique where all facts and dimensions are combined into a
single wide [[id:5636a488-dd17-4a92-befd-d16eaa0a526d][denormalized]] table.

- Massive storage due to denormalization can be offset be columnar formats e.g.
  Parquet which compress repeated values
  
* Pros

- Faster read performance because no need for expensive join operations
- Less sophisticated queries (no joins)
   
* Cons

- Single point of failure: Building of OBT is typically performed by a single
  monolithic scheduled pipeline. Edits to pipeline, e.g. adding joins to include
  a new column, may cause the entire OBT build to fail.

- Shared blast radius: If many consumers share the single OBT, they are all
  exposed to all deploys because all edits go through the one pipeline.

* Use Case

- Rapid prototyping/MVP
