#+title: Publish-Subscribe Pattern

[[id:7273653b-d9c9-4fc1-a575-47fc688ecc6f][Messaging]] pattern where message senders called publishers categorize messages
into [[id:a84e29a7-99cb-4261-a1bb-a03074f842bb][topics]] and recipients (subscribers) subscribe to said topics.
