#+title: Let's Encrypt

Non-profit [[id:46158213-90f2-476a-bde0-c74d94e0f7a1][Certificate Authority]] that provides free [[id:3d819ad3-802d-4f5e-9639-bdd5231de2c8][certificates]] for TLS
encryption. Free and automates issuance and renewal via the ACME (Automated Certificate
Management Environment) protocol.
